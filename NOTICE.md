# Notice: third-party content and credits

## What these images are
All 413 images under `img/` are frames captured from public gameplay videos (YouTube) of third-party Roblox games. They were collected as visual-quality references for AI agents and for game-development study: to compare the look of a developer's own game (UI, typography, lighting, effects, models, mobile layout) with well-made examples. Each line of `INDEX.md` says what the frame is a good example of. The videos and their creators are listed in `README.md` (sections "Source videos").
